Alzheimer's Detection via EEG

Working Paper / Ongoing
Objective:
Develop highly accurate Machine Learning models for the early, non-invasive detection of Alzheimer's disease using raw Electroencephalogram (EEG) data.
Methodology:
Bridging biomedical signal processing and deep neural networks to isolate cognitive degradation biomarkers within brainwave frequencies.
